FAQ

Common questions

Is this just ChatGPT with a nice interface?
No. CopyKraft AI uses a purpose-built system specifically optimised for marketing copy formats. Each tool has its own prompt engineering tuned for that exact format — ad copy, subject lines, cold outreach. You get structured, ready-to-use variants, not a wall of text.
Do I need copywriting experience to use it?
That's exactly the point — no, you don't. Just fill in your brand name, product, audience, and tone. CopyKraft AI handles the rest. It's built for people who hate writing marketing copy, not people who love it.
How is the free plan limited?
The free plan gives you 20 generations, access to 3 copy tools (Ad Copy, Email Subjects, Social Posts), and 2 variants per generation. It's enough to genuinely experience the product and see if it's right for you.
Can I cancel my subscription?
Yes, any time. There are no annual commitments or cancellation fees. You keep access until the end of your billing period.
Who is this for?
Solopreneurs, coaches, consultants, freelancers, small business owners — anyone who needs great marketing copy regularly but doesn't want to pay $500/hr for a copywriter or spend hours doing it themselves.
